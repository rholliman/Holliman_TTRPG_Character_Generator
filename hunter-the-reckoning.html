What You're Walking Into

You are not a vampire, not a werewolf, not a mage. You are just a person — a nurse, a mechanic, a grad student, a bartender — who saw something that shouldn't exist and couldn't un-see it. No one gave you powers. No one is coming to save you. What you have is grit, whatever gear you can get your hands on, and the other people who saw it too.

That shared mission is called the Vigil, and the small group you carry it out with is your cell. Hunting monsters as an ordinary human is not a fair fight, and this game is built to make sure you feel that. Every session is a negotiation between how far you're willing to go and how much of yourself you're willing to lose to go there.

How the Dice Work

Roll a pool of ten-sided dice equal to the relevant Attribute plus Skill. Any die that comes up 6 or higher is a success; you need enough successes to beat the difficulty your GM sets.

Your Creed lets you add bonus Desperation dice in situations that fit how you hunt. They're powerful, but risky — for every 1 that comes up on a Desperation die, your cell's Danger rating climbs, meaning your quarry gets more aware and more dangerous. And if you fail a roll even after spending Desperation dice, you fall into Despair and lose access to them entirely — until something tied to your Drive, your core reason for hunting, pulls you back.

Choose Your Creed

Your Creed isn't a superpower or a faction badge — it's your method. It determines exactly when you're allowed to spend Desperation dice.

Who Are They?

Your concept is your life before this, plus what it's become. A few examples:
• ER nurse who now stitches up wounds she can't explain to anyone
• Retired cop who never stopped noticing what didn't add up
• College kid whose roommate vanished and everyone else forgot she existed
• Pastor whose faith got a lot more literal one night
• Mechanic who fixes cars for the cell because it's the one thing that still makes sense
Your Drive is the real reason you keep doing this. A few examples:
• Vengeance — someone or something took from you, and you intend to make that even
• Curiosity — you need to understand what's actually out there, whatever it costs
• Redemption — you failed someone once, and this is how you live with it
• Duty — you made a promise, to a person, a faith, or an institution
• Protection — there's a person or a place you've sworn will not be next

Attributes

Attributes come in three groups: Physical, Social, and Mental. Rank the groups below — your top priority gets 6 dots to spread among its three Attributes, your second gets 4, your third gets 3. Every Attribute starts at 1 automatically, and none can go above 4 at creation.

Skills

Skills work the same way. Rank Physical, Social, and Mental again (you can rank them differently than you did for Attributes) — top priority gets 11 dots, second gets 7, third gets 4. Skills start at 0, and none can go above 3 at creation.

Touchstone

A person, place, or routine that still keeps your Hunter connected to their humanity. If they lose it, they lose more than comfort — they risk losing themselves to the Vigil.

A few ideas: a little sister who still thinks you're just tired all the time · Sunday dinner with your parents · a dog who doesn't care what you saw last night · the community garden you still show up to water · an old friend who has no idea any of this is real
